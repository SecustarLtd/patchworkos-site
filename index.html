Governed Autonomy

The Four Pillars of Supervised AI Work

Deploy agents with complete peace of mind across your codebase, communications, operations, and infrastructure.

1. Ask Before Acting (Blast-Radius Gating)

Every action is classified by blast radius: can it be undone, and how much breaks if it's wrong? Reversible queries and diagnostics run immediately. Risky actions—code commits, database mutations, customer emails—wait in the Approval Queue with evidence attached.

2. Trust is Earned, Never Assumed (Autonomy Ramp)

Workers—named agents with defined jobs—start strictly supervised. They earn autonomy gradually through a verifiable track record per action type. Good at filing issues ≠ allowed to push code. One bad call outweighs a hundred successes. You always hold the ceiling.

3. Every Decision Leaves a Receipt (Audit Traces)

What was done, why it was allowed, and how it turned out. Durable, replayable, and explainable via the traces dashboard and patchwork judgments. When you approve an action, you find out later whether you were right.

4. 100% Local-First & Zero Vendor Lock-In

All recipes, credentials, connector tokens, logs, and receipts live locally under ~/.patchwork. No third-party servers sit in your execution path. Connect to Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or fully air-gapped local Ollama models.
